# SYNTHETIC buy-side DD skill package

Use Stage 3 data-room review as a demanding workbench example. This is a rehearsal model, not a complete acquisition or approved firm policy.

The partner allocates review areas and processing scope. The associate checks whether AI use is permitted, loads permitted documents and the supplied playbook, verifies AI-prepared findings across review areas, and performs separate register searches. The partner assesses consolidated red flags. The buyer may proceed, require protections, or withdraw. AI prepares RFIs; the associate edits them; the partner approves or returns them. An integration task represents posting to the VDR, followed by waiting for target responses, updating findings and partner handoff review.

Model parallel verification, explicit refusal, manual fallback, missing response, bounded repeated RFI rounds and withheld final sign-off. Treat three RFI rounds and handoff to Stage 5 as synthetic policy assumptions, not firm requirements. The fixture does not resolve the role of Stage 4. A real package must confirm these before operational reuse.

Inputs: matter reference, source inventory, scope, supplied playbook and VDR processing terms. Outputs: inventory, candidate issues, verified findings, RFIs, response updates and sign-off outcome. The current fixture uses synthetic references to test routing, not a real data-room ingestion service. No party names, client material, production credentials or external sends are required.

The model is adapted from the user's prior local DD rehearsal. Generated methods should preserve source locators, competing accounts, missing evidence and the boundary between preparation and legal judgment. The host performs substantive work on invocation; the CLI must not claim to infer legal conclusions from this brief.
